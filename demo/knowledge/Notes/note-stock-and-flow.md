A stock is what exists right now; a flow is what changes it. Almost every
argument about a system is really an argument about which of the two you are
looking at. Water in the bath, and the taps.

The useful move is asking which one you can actually touch. See
[[note-delays-cause-oscillation]] for what happens when you can only touch
the flow and the stock answers slowly.
